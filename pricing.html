A few useful answers

What’s free, and what needs a membership?

The 17 resources and 4 printable downloads are free, without an account. The 50+ condition and skills study guides and their attached practice quizzes require a study membership.

Can I try a full study guide for free?

The study guides and their quizzes currently require a membership. You can read all of the free resources before deciding to join.

Do you cover my whole nursing program?

The library currently covers selected topics in Fundamentals, Medical-Surgical Nursing, Maternal-Newborn, Pediatrics, and Pharmacology. Coverage varies by class. Browse the available guides before subscribing.

How do billing and cancellation work?

The study membership is $14.99 USD per month and renews until canceled. Cancel from Account → Plan & Billing. You keep access until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

I already have another plan. What changes for me?

Your current membership and purchased benefits remain in place, including any existing semester, lifetime, or AI plan. Use Account to manage your plan and find Additional tools. Contact support if you need help.

What if the membership isn’t right for me?

Contact us within 7 days of purchase to request a refund under the existing refund policy.
